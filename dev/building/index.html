Building blocks

The estimators of this package are assembled from a few pieces that other packages can use as well, such as the varying-coefficient models of CoordinateDescent.jl: the kernel weights of the observations near a point, the default evaluation points and bandwidth grids, the search for the minimizer of a criterion over a grid, the robust variances of a local weighted least squares fit, and the critical value of uniform bands. Their results agree with those inside the estimators.

d = SmoothingData(x, y)
k = EpanechnikovKernel()
grid = bandwidth_grid(d; mindistinct = 3)          # local linear fits are identified on it
h, details = grid_minimize(cvcriterion, grid)      # a criterion of your own, a function of h
r = kernel_weights(d, k, 0.5, h)
Z = [ones(length(r.window)) (d.x[r.window] .- 0.5) ./ h]
θ = (Z' * (r.weights .* Z)) \ (Z' * (r.weights .* d.y[r.window]))
V = sandwich(Z, r.weights, d.y[r.window] .- Z * θ; vce = HCVariance(3))
KernelSmoothing.kernel_weights — Function
kernel_weights(d::SmoothingData, k::AbstractKernel, x0, h)

The observations that the nonnegative kernel k weights at the point x0 with the bandwidth h, and their weights wᵢ k((xᵢ - x0)/h), where wᵢ are the observation weights of d (one without). Returns a named tuple of window, the range of sorted observations from the first to the last with positive weight, index, their indices in the order of the input (d.perm[window]), and weights. The weights omit the factor 1/h of Kₕ(t) = k(t/h)/h, which cancels in a local fit. The window is empty when no observation has positive weight. Takes O(log n) time and the length of the window.

Examples

d = SmoothingData(x, y)
r = kernel_weights(d, EpanechnikovKernel(), 0.5, 0.2)
Z = [ones(length(r.window)) (d.x[r.window] .- 0.5) ./ 0.2]   # a local linear design
θ = (Z' * (r.weights .* Z)) \ (Z' * (r.weights .* d.y[r.window]))

KernelSmoothing.regression_points — Function
regression_points(d::SmoothingData; compat = false)

The default evaluation points of the regression estimators: 30 equally spaced points between the quantiles of order 0.025 and 0.975 of x, which keeps them out of sparse tails, or between the extremes of x with compat = true, as nprobust, and where these quantiles coincide.

KernelSmoothing.bandwidth_grid — Function
bandwidth_grid(d::SmoothingData; minobs = 2, mindistinct = 2, gridsize = 30)

gridsize bandwidths spaced evenly on the log scale, from the smallest one at which the window of every observation holds at least minobs observations (itself included, at most all of them) and mindistinct distinct values of x, increased by one percent, to the range of x (or from half the range, where the smallest one exceeds it). Below the lower end, the local fit at some observation has too few observations or distinct values to be identified, as for a fit of order p with mindistinct ≤ p, so that a cross-validation criterion is not finite there.

KernelSmoothing.grid_minimize — Function
grid_minimize(f, grid; largest = false, rtol = 1e-3)

The minimizer of the criterion f over the increasing grid, refined by a golden-section search between the neighbours of the minimizer on the grid, to the relative tolerance rtol, where both neighbours have finite values and the refined point has a value no larger. With largest = true, the largest interior local minimizer whose value is within half of the lowest interior local minimum replaces the global one, as in Sheather's rule for least-squares cross-validation. Values that are not finite count as Inf, and an error is thrown when none is. Returns (h, details), where details holds the grid, the criterion on it, the interior local minimizers, and atbound, whether the minimizer is an end of the grid, beyond which the minimum may lie.

KernelSmoothing.sandwich — Function
sandwich(Z, v, e; vce = HCVariance(0), cluster = nothing)

The robust covariance matrix of the weighted least squares coefficients θ̂ = (ZᵀVZ)⁻¹ZᵀVy of a local fit, from its design Z, one row per observation, its weights v (the kernel weights times the observation weights, nonnegative) and its residuals e,

c (ZᵀVZ)⁻¹ (∑_g s_g s_gᵀ) (ZᵀVZ)⁻¹,   s_g = ∑_{i ∈ g} vᵢ ε̃ᵢ zᵢ,

where g runs over the clusters, or over the observations without them, and only observations with positive weight enter. With vce = HCVariance(type), the residuals ε̃ᵢ and the factor c are those of HCVariance, with the leverages hᵢᵢ = vᵢ zᵢᵀ(ZᵀVZ)⁻¹zᵢ of this fit and n its number of observations with positive weight. With vce = ClusterVariance(type) and the labels cluster, one per row, they are those of ClusterVariance, with the blocks H_gg of the hat matrix Z(ZᵀVZ)⁻¹ZᵀV. When Z holds the powers of (xᵢ - x₀)/h, the first entry is the squared standard error of the conventional estimate of LocalPolynomialRegression at x₀ with the same variance estimator.

The weights enter the middle of the sandwich squared, the convention for sampling weights; for frequency weights, which stand for replicated observations, the middle holds wᵢ (kᵢ ε̃ᵢ)² instead. The matrix is NaN when ZᵀVZ is singular, and its entries are NaN where the variance estimator cannot be computed, as at a leverage of one or with fewer than two clusters.

KernelSmoothing.supt_critical — Function
supt_critical(C; level = 0.95, draws = 10_000, rng = Random.default_rng())

The critical value of uniform bands for estimates with the covariance matrix C: the level quantile of maxⱼ |Zⱼ| / σⱼ for Z ~ N(0, C) and σⱼ² = Cⱼⱼ, by simulation from draws draws, kept between the pointwise and the Bonferroni critical values, between which the exact one lies. Estimates with zero or NaN variance are left out, and with one estimate the pointwise value is exact. This is the critical value of conf_band.
